Solid golden ground: stability, property and a lasting foundation. What you build now will hold, though results come slowly and ask for patience.

Advice

Build slowly on what is solid.

Practice

Make offerings to the land you live on, and keep your promises.

The symbol

Gold is the color of the earth element in many Tibetan teachings, and the golden ground is the firm base on which a mandala is built. Nothing is raised on it yet; what it offers is a foundation that will not give way.

The two throws

The Ground of Gold comes up when the first throw of the die shows A (space) and the second shows NA (earth).

ཨ
First: A · Space

A is the first sound of every alphabet and the syllable of open space. It stands for what is wide, clear and not yet filled: room to move, a fresh start, nothing in the way.

ན
Second: NA · Earth

NA is the syllable of earth. It stands for what is solid and lasting: land, home, property, the body and slow, steady increase.

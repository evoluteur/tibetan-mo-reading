Divination 29 of 36 · NA NA Good

A mansion built of gold

securityhomecomfortestablishment

A mansion of gold: security, comfort and a well-established home. Everything has its place. Enjoy it, but do not let comfort make you rigid.

Advice

Make your home a place of welcome.

Practice

Bless your home and make offerings there.

The symbol

A mansion of gold is the palace of a prosperous household, and also recalls the celestial palaces of deities in a mandala. It stands for a secure place where everything is in order and welcome.

The two throws

The Mansion of Gold comes up when the first throw of the die shows NA (earth) and the second shows NA (earth).

First: NA · Earth

NA is the syllable of earth. It stands for what is solid and lasting: land, home, property, the body and slow, steady increase.

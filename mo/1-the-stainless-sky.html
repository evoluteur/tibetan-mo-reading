A clear sky without a single cloud

clarityopennessfresh startno obstacles

A clear sky: no clouds, no obstacles. The matter is open and clean, though not much is moving yet. It is a good time to begin again from an honest, uncluttered place.

Advice

Keep your mind as open as the sky, and act without clutter.

Practice

Recite Manjushri's mantra and rest in open awareness.

The symbol

The open sky is a classic Tibetan image for the nature of mind: clouds of thought come and go, but the sky itself is never stained by them. A sky with no clouds at all is a sign that nothing obstructs the matter, for better and for worse: there is space, but it still has to be filled.

The two throws

The Stainless Sky comes up when the first throw of the die shows A (space) and the second shows A (space).

ཨ
First: A · Space

A is the first sound of every alphabet and the syllable of open space. It stands for what is wide, clear and not yet filled: room to move, a fresh start, nothing in the way.

A blazing weapon

decisivenessvictorycourageforce

A blazing weapon: the power to cut through obstacles decisively. Victory comes through bold action, but force can wound, so aim it only at the problem.

Practice

Recite the mantra of Vajrapani to clear obstacles.

The symbol

Wrathful protectors in Tibetan art carry flaming weapons: swords, hooks and lassos wreathed in fire. The weapon is aimed at obstacles and ignorance, not at people, which is the spirit of this sign: decisive, but precise.

The two throws

The Great Fiery Weapon comes up when the first throw of the die shows TSA (wind) and the second shows RA (fire).

ཙ
First: TSA · Wind

TSA is the syllable of wind. It carries movement, breath, messages and travel. It spreads news and fans flames, and it can scatter what is not tied down.

ར
Second: RA · Fire

RA is the syllable of fire. It brings energy, warmth, light and the power to make things happen, and with them the risks of fire: haste, anger and burning out.

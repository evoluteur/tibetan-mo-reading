Wind scattering the earth

Wind scatters the earth: the body and everyday circumstances become the obstacle. Illness, fatigue and practical setbacks get in the way. Look after the basics before anything else.

The symbol

The Mara of the Aggregates is the third of the four maras: the body and the other components of a person, which bring illness, aging and limits of every kind. Wind scattering earth is the body and everyday life losing their steadiness.

The two throws

The Mara Demon of the Aggregates comes up when the first throw of the die shows TSA (wind) and the second shows NA (earth).

ཙ
First: TSA · Wind

TSA is the syllable of wind. It carries movement, breath, messages and travel. It spreads news and fans flames, and it can scatter what is not tied down.

ན
Second: NA · Earth

NA is the syllable of earth. It stands for what is solid and lasting: land, home, property, the body and slow, steady increase.

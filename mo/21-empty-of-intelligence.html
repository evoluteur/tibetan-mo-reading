Divination 21 of 36 · TSA PA Difficult

Spray scattered by the wind

confusionfollydeceptiondistraction

A mind scattered like spray in the wind: poor judgment, confusion and deception. Plans made now are likely to be unwise, and others may mislead you. Ask for advice and do not commit.

Advice

Do not trust your first plan. Ask someone wise.

Practice

Recite Manjushri's mantra for wisdom, and study.

The symbol

Spray thrown up by the wind has no shape and goes nowhere. The name of this divination is blunt about what that means for a plan: there is no intelligence in it yet. It is one of the few signs that tells you to doubt yourself.

The two throws

Empty of Intelligence comes up when the first throw of the die shows TSA (wind) and the second shows PA (water).

ཙ
First: TSA · Wind

TSA is the syllable of wind. It carries movement, breath, messages and travel. It spreads news and fans flames, and it can scatter what is not tied down.

པ
Second: PA · Water

PA is the syllable of water. It soothes, nourishes and heals, and it carries feeling and enjoyment. Disturbed, water turns murky; dried up, it leaves nothing behind.

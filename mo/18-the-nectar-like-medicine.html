The symbol

Tibetan medicine, Sowa Rigpa, is closely tied to the Medicine Buddha, who holds a bowl of nectar and a healing plant. Medicine that is like nectar both cures and nourishes, so the sign speaks of healing that leaves things better than before.

The two throws

The Nectar-like Medicine comes up when the first throw of the die shows PA (water) and the second shows DHI (wisdom).

པ
First: PA · Water

PA is the syllable of water. It soothes, nourishes and heals, and it carries feeling and enjoyment. Disturbed, water turns murky; dried up, it leaves nothing behind.

དྷཱི
Second: DHI · Wisdom

DHI is the seed syllable of Manjushri, the bodhisattva of wisdom. It stands for clear understanding, intelligence and blessing, and it turns almost any throw toward a good outcome.

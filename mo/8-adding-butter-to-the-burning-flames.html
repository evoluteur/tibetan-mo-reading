Divination 8 of 36 · RA RA Good

Butter poured onto a fire that is already burning

growthmomentumpassionexcess

Butter on a fire: whatever is already burning grows fast. Efforts gain momentum and success multiplies, but so do anger and excess. Feed what you want to grow, and only that.

Strong energy. Put it into devotion, not pride.

Found quickly, perhaps near the kitchen or the hearth.

Advice

Feed only the fire you want.

Practice

Light lamps, and calm your anger before you act.

The symbol

Butter fuels the lamps and the fire offerings of Tibetan ritual. Poured onto a fire that is already burning, it makes the flames leap up at once. The image is about increase: what is already under way grows stronger and faster.

The two throws

Adding Butter to the Burning Flames comes up when the first throw of the die shows RA (fire) and the second shows RA (fire).

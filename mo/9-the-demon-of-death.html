Divination 9 of 36 · RA PA Caution

Fire and water clashing

dangerendingcautionobstacle

Fire and water clash. This sign warns of danger and of something coming to an end. It rarely means a literal death, but it asks for great care: postpone risky plans and protect what is fragile.

The Demon of Death by question

Home & family
Discord, or worry about an elder. Look after the household.
Plans & wishes
Not now. The plan meets serious obstacles.
Wealth & friends
Risk of loss. Do not lend or invest.
Rivals & disputes
Your opponents are strong. Avoid confrontation.
Guests & travel
Postpone the journey if you can. Visitors may be delayed.
Health
Take any illness seriously and get proper care quickly.
Spiritual practice
Practice for long life and purification.
Lost things
Unlikely to be recovered.

Advice

Slow down, protect what matters, and let what is ending end.

Practice

Do long-life practices and save lives, for instance by freeing animals.

The symbol

The Demon of Death is the first of the four maras of Buddhist teaching, the forces that obstruct the path: death itself, here pictured as fire and water destroying each other. The traditional response to this sign is not fear but protection and merit, especially practices for long life.

The two throws

The Demon of Death comes up when the first throw of the die shows RA (fire) and the second shows PA (water).

ར
First: RA · Fire

RA is the syllable of fire. It brings energy, warmth, light and the power to make things happen, and with them the risks of fire: haste, anger and burning out.

པ
Second: PA · Water

PA is the syllable of water. It soothes, nourishes and heals, and it carries feeling and enjoyment. Disturbed, water turns murky; dried up, it leaves nothing behind.

The sound of the vajra, which nothing can break: unshakable clarity. Your words carry weight, protection surrounds you, and decisions made now are sound. One of the best signs for anything that needs firmness and truth.

The symbol

The vajra, the ritual scepter of Tibetan Buddhism, stands for what cannot be broken or cut, and its name also means diamond. The sound of the vajra suggests speech with that same quality: true, firm and impossible to bend.

The two throws

The Tone of Vajras comes up when the first throw of the die shows A (space) and the second shows DHI (wisdom).

ཨ
First: A · Space

A is the first sound of every alphabet and the syllable of open space. It stands for what is wide, clear and not yet filled: room to move, a fresh start, nothing in the way.

དྷཱི
Second: DHI · Wisdom

DHI is the seed syllable of Manjushri, the bodhisattva of wisdom. It stands for clear understanding, intelligence and blessing, and it turns almost any throw toward a good outcome.

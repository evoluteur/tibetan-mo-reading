The symbol

In a culture of travel by caravan, news arrived with guests, and good news was cause for celebration. A house of good tidings is a home where the messenger brings what you hoped to hear.

The two throws

The House of Good Tidings comes up when the first throw of the die shows TSA (wind) and the second shows DHI (wisdom).

ཙ
First: TSA · Wind

TSA is the syllable of wind. It carries movement, breath, messages and travel. It spreads news and fans flames, and it can scatter what is not tied down.

དྷཱི
Second: DHI · Wisdom

DHI is the seed syllable of Manjushri, the bodhisattva of wisdom. It stands for clear understanding, intelligence and blessing, and it turns almost any throw toward a good outcome.
